👥 Human Resources

Member Management

Manage the complete lifecycle of your organization's personnel from intake to profile intelligence.

A high-density registry of every verified member with full search and filtering capabilities.

Member Types

Define categories like 'General', 'Life', or 'Executive' to segment your member base for reporting.

A vetting station for incoming registrations. Review, verify, and migrate data to the active registry.

💰 Financials

Fund Management

Transparent tracking of organizational capital with multi-stage approval workflows.

Disbursement Workflow

Track funds from Pending to Approved to Disbursed. Each entry requires a confirmed 'Disbursement Date' for audit logging.

Reference & Accountability

Link every transaction to a Member ID and a Reference Number. Ensure all entries have bilingual titles for public reporting.

Audit Ready: The system maintains a permanent record of all financial outflows, categorized by purpose and recipient.

Asset Requests

A checkout system to track which members are currently utilizing specific organization property in the field.
