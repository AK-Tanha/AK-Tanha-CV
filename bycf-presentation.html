The Three Pillars

Your administrative suite is divided into three distinct operational domains.

Human Resources

Verification, Member Types, Committee Roles, and Academic/Personal profile intelligence.

Capital & Logistics

Financial workflows (Donations/Funds) and Asset Inventory (Requests/Tracking).

Public Outreach

Events, Activities, Social Connectors (YouTube), Media Gallery, and Bilingual Blog content.

👥 Human Resources

Member Intake Workflow

3

💰 Capital (Outbound)

Fund Disbursement

Manage organizational spending through a multi-stage approval workflow.

A

Approval Workflow

Funds progress from Pending to Approved to Disbursed. Actual payouts are only logged once the disbursement date is confirmed.

B

Reference Registry

Track funds by Reference Number and Purpose. Each fund is linked to a Member ID for clear accountability.

Bilingual Fund Data: Every fund entry requires a Bangla title and purpose to ensure financial transparency on the public portal.

Asset Requests

🌐 Outreach

Bilingual Content Management

Your site uses a 1:1 sync engine. Content must exist in both languages for the best user experience.

Blog Posts

Create rich-text stories with the built-in Jodit editor. Manage featured images and category slugs.

Notices (Priority)

Mark urgent notices as High Priority to pin them to the top of the public website.

Slug Standard: Always use lowercase and hyphens for links (e.g., annual-general-meeting-2026). Do not use spaces or special characters.
